Modern Dilated Time Models (TCN over LSTM): Using a Temporal Convolutional Network instead of standard RNNs/LSTMs is smart. TCNs parallelize significantly better, don't suffer from vanishing gradients, and use dilated convolutions to achieve massive receptive fields (e.g., observing 127 days back without losing short-term memory).
Tackling Non-Stationarity Directly: Financial data is notoriously non-stationary (the rules change constantly). Your inclusion of Blackwell Regime Detection combined with TDA (Topological Data Analysis) is a highly advanced way to detect market phase shifts (e.g., from a momentum-driven bull market to a high-volatility mean-reverting market) that standard LSTMs miss. TDA is cutting-edge for detecting market crashes/transitions before they fully materialize in standard volatility metrics.
CausalFolio is safer. RL in finance often fails because the agent discovers "hacks" in the simulator that don't exist in reality. Your approach (predicting returns/direction, then using a deterministic Risk/Kelly execution layer) is much closer to actual institutional practice.
